Right-aligned is not right-to-left A Persian line that starts with an English term is still Persian — most fixes get this wrong.

✕ dir="rtl" alone — you have to read from the left

  1. Orpheus TTS 3B — احتمالاً فارسی native داره (Llama 3.2 tokenizer)

  2. OmniVoice — بهترین گزینه (native، ۶۴۶ زبان)

✓ Parsi RTL — reads right-to-left, as written

  1. Orpheus TTS 3B — احتمالاً فارسی native داره (Llama 3.2 tokenizer)

  2. OmniVoice — بهترین گزینه (native، ۶۴۶ زبان)

The direction of a line comes from its language, not from its first character. Parsi RTL decides per block by script ratio — and never touches your code blocks.
